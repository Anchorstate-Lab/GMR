# What GMR sells is one relation, and everything else is sediment

The product is a single primitive: an "about" relation between a claim and a
coordinate in reality that a third party can recompute — this claim is bound to
these anchors, the ground under them moved or held, the claim was built from
that reading or it was not. GMR is not a memory store, not retrieval, not
citation checking, and not a truth engine ([[gmr-not-entailment]]). Hallucination
reduction, the knowledge network, the minimal information set — those are
sediment left by many such relations being written and asked, never features to
enumerate. When they fail to appear the relation was designed badly, not
under-featured.

## Three consumption depths, one δ

Reading the relation once answers a one-shot question. Subscribing to its
transitions is the coding domain's whole loop — `check`, hand back, `accept`
with a sealed reason. Having it arrive before an answer's minimal logical
closure forms is what interrupts a wrong answer that was about to be internally
consistent. These are depths of use of the same state machine, not product
surfaces, and the layer boundaries between fact, memory and inference stay as
[[three-layers]] draws them at every depth.

## The economics at the moment of closure

When a model closes its loop it can guess — free, immediate, self-consistent —
or check, which costs knowing what to ask and where. Everything GMR is for
reduces to moving that relative price. Two halves, and losing either sends the
consumer back to guessing: the answer at a coordinate must carry only what is
*about* it (a delivery that mixes in what merely mentions it re-dilutes the
attention it was meant to focus), and asking must stay cheap at the single
coordinate (a per-key question that bills the whole repository prices the honest
path out of hot loops).

## The fourth refusal

Beside the three the base already makes — no semantic truth verdicts, no
choosing anchors for the author, no intercepting answers — a fourth: GMR does
not compute a minimal information set. The objective, "the closure comes out
correct", depends on the task, and the set would have to be chosen before the
task is known. Topology can make hints cheap; it cannot decide.

## Earned is falsifiable

The network is earned when authors write links whose kind they mean and when
the anchors on working code can reach the notes that say what the system is.
Both are countable: authored edges in the link store, and the share of code
anchors that can deliver a positioning note. Zero on either is a measurement of
the design, not a request for more features.

## When this changes, ask

Does the Abstract still say the runtime reports structure, never entailment,
and still refuse the truth-engine reading? Does 2.4 still name the grounding
relationship — not the memory, the fact, or the probe — as the unit of
reliability? If either section moved away from that, this note is due against
it, and the question is whether the product changed or the document did.
